Responses & Analytics now always shows the report for the form you picked last. Clicking a second form while the first was still loading could leave the first form's numbers on screen under the second form's name, depending on which load finished first. The form list is no longer locked while a report loads, so you can move straight on to another form, and the previous form's figures clear as soon as you do.
