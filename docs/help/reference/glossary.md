# Glossary

Every word the help centre uses that you might not already have a meaning for, in the sense MJ Forms
means it. Terms are in alphabetical order.

## Answer

What one person gave for one question. A response is made up of answers, one per question they
filled in.

## Automation

Something MJ Forms does for you each time someone submits — create or update a record, run an
action, or hand the submission to an AI agent. You set these up on the **Automate** tab. The answers
are saved first, always, so an automation that fails never costs you a submission.

## Captcha

A short check that proves a respondent is a person rather than a script. You switch it on for one
share link with **Require a captcha**. It only works if someone technical has set the keys up on the
server first; without them, every submission through that link is refused.

## Draft

A form that has never been published, and also the edits you have made since the last time you
published. A draft is visible only to you and your colleagues — nobody answering the form ever sees
it.

## Embed

Showing your form inside a page of your own website, instead of sending people to its own address.
The **Embed** view on the **Distribute** tab gives you the snippet to paste into that page.

## Ending screen

The screen a respondent sees after they submit. A form can have more than one, and logic decides who
lands on which. If you add none, respondents see a short confirmation instead.

## Form

The questions you ask, how they look, the rules about who sees what, and what happens when someone
submits. One form collects one set of responses.

## Insights

The summary of what a form's answers add up to — how many people finished, where they stopped, what
they chose and what they wrote. It is one of the two views on the **Responses & Analytics**
dashboard; the other is the responses themselves, one by one.

## Logic

Rules that decide when a question appears and where a respondent goes after answering it. Rules are
checked in order and the first one that matches wins.

## Partial response

What you have from someone who started your form and did not finish. MJ Forms saves one on its own
as a respondent answers, whether or not you asked for it; **Save progress here** banks a page the
moment they leave it rather than waiting for that to come round.

A partial response is not a row on the **Responses** tab and is not in the export. Where you see
these people is the count of how many started and did not finish, and the chart of where they
stopped: [Read the insights](../responses/insights.md).

## Preview

A rehearsal of the form exactly as a respondent will see it, at desktop, tablet or phone width.
Nothing you do in a preview is recorded as a response.

## Published

Live. Publishing takes the form as it stands and makes that the one respondents get. Anything you
change afterwards stays private until you publish again.

## QR code

A square pattern that opens your share link when somebody points a phone camera at it. You download
it from the **QR code** view on the **Distribute** tab. It downloads as a vector file, so it stays
sharp at any print size.

## Question

One thing you ask. Every question has a type, and the type decides what a respondent can enter, what
the answer is checked against, and which keyboard their phone offers them.

## Record

Something your organisation already keeps track of — a person, an organisation, an application. An
automation can turn each submission into one, so that the rest of your system can find it, report on
it and act on it.

## Required

A question a respondent cannot move past without answering. You set it per question, with
**Required**.

## Respondent

The person filling in your form. They do not sign in, and they do not need an account of any kind.

## Response

Everything one respondent sent you: their answers, when they submitted, and which published version
of the form they were answering.

## Response limit

The number of responses a share link accepts before it stops taking any more. Leave it empty and
there is no limit. It is set per link, so two links to the same form can have different limits.

## Score

Points you attach to particular answers. The running total can decide which ending screen someone
lands on, and automations can act on it. Respondents never see their score.

## Section

A group of questions shown together as one page. Respondents move through a form one section at a
time, with a **Back** and a **Next** button.

## Share link

The web address you send people so they can open and answer your form, without signing in. One form
can have several, each with its own settings and its own count of responses — one for the newsletter
and one for the printed poster, so you can tell which worked.

## Template

A saved copy of a whole form — its questions, pages, logic, validation, welcome and ending screens,
look and automations — that you can start a new form from. Responses and share links are not copied.

## Validation

A rule that rejects an answer which is not in the shape you need, and tells the respondent why. A
postcode that is not a postcode, an answer under ten characters.

## Version

Every time you publish, MJ Forms records the form exactly as it stood at that moment. Each response
remembers which version it was answering, so editing a question later never changes what somebody
was actually asked.

## Welcome screen

An optional screen shown before the first question, with a title, a description and a button to
begin. Use it to say who is asking and how long the form takes.
