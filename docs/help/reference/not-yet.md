# What MJ Forms cannot do yet

The things authors go looking for and do not find. Every one of them is genuinely absent rather than
hidden somewhere you have not thought to look, so this page exists to stop you looking.

There are no dates here, because none of these has one. Where there is something to do instead, it
is on the same line.

## Approval before publishing

Anyone who can edit a form can publish it. There is no review step, no second pair of eyes and no
way to hold a version until somebody signs it off. *Instead:* settle who presses **Publish** before
anybody starts writing, and use [Preview on phone and tablet](../design/preview.md) as the check.

## A Payment question type

No question takes money. [The 25 question types](../build/question-types.md) is the whole list.
*Instead:* send people to whatever your organisation already uses to take payment, with
**Redirect after submit** on an ending screen —
[Add sections and screens](../build/sections-and-screens.md).

## Projecting responses into a queryable view

Responses do not become a table of their own that reports and queries elsewhere in MemberJunction
can read directly. *Instead:* [Save answers into a record](../automate/save-into-a-record.md) writes
each submission into a record the rest of your system already queries, which is what most people
want this for.

## AI answer scores shown in the interface

One of the built-in steps has AI read the written answers. What it writes per answer is not shown
anywhere in MJ Forms and is deliberately left out of the export. *Instead:* the recurring words under
**What they wrote** are the nearest thing — [Read the insights](../responses/insights.md).

## Calculated fields

No question's answer can be worked out from other answers, and no answer can be added to, converted
or totalled on the form. *Instead:* the one running total MJ Forms keeps is the score, and it decides
which ending screen somebody lands on — [Score answers](../build/scoring.md).

## Quotas beyond a simple response limit

A share link stops at a number of responses and that is the only cap there is. Nothing caps one
option of one question — *twenty places on Saturday, twenty on Sunday*. *Instead:* give each audience
its own link with its own limit, if you can keep them apart —
[Set limits and dates](../share/limits-and-dates.md).

## Resuming a half-finished response on another device

Answers come back on the browser and the device they were typed on, and nowhere else. There is no
email carrying somebody back into a part-finished response, on any device. *Instead:* keep a form
short enough to finish in one sitting, and tell respondents to finish where they started —
[Questions respondents ask](respondent-questions.md) says it in their words.

## Reading a half-finished response

A response somebody started and did not finish is saved, but it is not a row you can open, filter to
or export. What you get is the count and the chart of where people stopped —
[Read responses](../responses/read-responses.md) explains where each of those lives.
