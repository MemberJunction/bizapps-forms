# Create your first form

A form of your own, published, with a link anyone can open — and your own answer to it, read back
from inside MJ Forms. About ten minutes.

## Before you start

- You can sign in to MemberJunction and there is a **Forms** card on the home page. If there is no
  card, MJ Forms is not installed for you yet — that is a job for whoever looks after your system.

## Steps

1. Open MemberJunction and find the **Forms** card on the home page. It carries two links; choose
   **Forms**. The other one, **Responses & Analytics**, is for reading responses across every form
   at once, and you do not need it today.
2. Press **New form**, at the top of the page beside **Templates** and **Author with AI**. MJ Forms
   creates the form and opens it immediately — nothing asks you for a name first.
3. Type a name over **Untitled form** in the box at the top left, then press Enter. The browser tab
   takes the same name, which is how you tell two open forms apart.
4. On the **Build** tab, in the list of question types down the left, find the **Contact** group and
   choose **Contact info**. The question lands on the page and MJ Forms selects it for you.
5. In **Question settings**, on the right, type what you want to ask into **Question** — *Your
   details*, for a first form.
6. Back in the list on the left, find the **Choice** group and choose **Multiple choice**.
7. In **Question settings**, type the second question into **Question**, then replace **Option 1**
   and **Option 2** with the answers you want to offer.
8. Press **Publish**, at the right-hand end of the bar above the tabs. There is no save button to
   press first — MJ Forms saved each edit as you made it, and publishing is the separate decision
   about what respondents get.
9. Read what that button says now — **Published, not shared**. Nothing has gone wrong. Publishing
   makes the form answerable; it does not hand anyone a way in. Press the button and MJ Forms opens
   the **Distribute** tab, which is where you fix that.
10. Press **Create a share link**. One link appears, marked **Live**, with its web address in the
    box beside **Copy link**. That address is what you would send people.
11. Press **Open it yourself**. Your form opens in a new tab exactly as a respondent gets it, with
    no sign-in and no account.
12. Answer both questions, then press **Submit**. A short thank-you replaces the form once the
    answer is in.
13. Go back to the builder tab and open the **Responses** tab.
14. Press **Refresh** if the table is still empty, then click your row to read the answer back.

## What happens next

Your form is published and its link keeps working. Every answer from now on arrives the same way,
and you read it in the same place.

- The **Respondent** column says **Anonymous**. That is correct: a
  [respondent](../reference/glossary.md) never signs in, so a name you collected is one of the
  answers rather than proof of who sent them.
- Editing from here changes nothing for anyone holding the link. The publish button turns into
  **Publish changes** while you are carrying edits nobody has seen, and each published version is
  kept — rewording a question later never changes what somebody was actually asked.
- Counting, charting and exporting responses happen on **Responses & Analytics**, not on this tab.
  [Where everything lives](where-everything-lives.md) says which surface does what, and why the
  export is not where most people look for it first.
