# Where everything lives

MJ Forms sits in two places in MemberJunction, and a form itself has five tabs. This page says which
one does what, so you spend your time in the right one.

## The two places

The **Forms** card on your MemberJunction home page carries two links, and they are separate on
purpose. Building is a job you do to one form. Reading responses is usually a job you do across all
of them.

| Link | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Forms** | Every form you have, and the place you build them. Open a form here to write questions, brand it, publish it, create links, and read responses one at a time. |
| **Responses & Analytics** | What came back. Pick a form from the list of every form that has been published, then read the numbers, the patterns and the individual responses — and export them. |

## Inside a form: five tabs

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Write the questions. Choose a type from the list on the left, set its wording and settings on the right, arrange it into pages, and decide who sees what. |
| **Design** | Make the form look like it came from you: a logo, a font, colours and a background. Changes here go live at the next publish, like everything else. |
| **Distribute** | Create share links and everything that hangs off one — the web address, a QR code, an embed snippet, response limits, closing dates and a captcha. |
| **Automate** | What happens each time someone submits, beyond keeping the answers: create or update a record, run an action, or hand the submission to an AI agent. |
| **Responses** | The responses to this form, one at a time, with what each person answered and what the submission set off. |

## The bar above the tabs

The bar stays put whichever tab you are on. From the left: the form's name, which you rename by
typing over it; **Scroll** and **One question**, which decide whether a respondent gets a page of
questions at a time or one question at a time; **Save as template**; **Preview**; and the publish
control.

That last control is the form's status as well as its button. It reads **Publish** before you have
ever published, **Publish changes** while you are carrying edits nobody has seen, and after a
successful publish it reports how far the form actually reaches — **Published** when a live link
exists, **Published, not shared** when none does. The second of those is where a first-time author
lands, and it is not a failure: see [Create your first form](first-form.md).

## The export is on the dashboard, not the Responses tab

The **Responses** tab in the builder shows you this form's responses one at a time. It has no export
button, and no amount of looking will find one.

The export is on **Responses & Analytics**, as two buttons in the header beside the name of the form
you have picked: **CSV** and **Excel**. The same header switches between **Insights**, which is what
the answers add up to, and **Responses**, which is the list of individual responses.

So *responses* names two surfaces. The tab is for reading one person's answers while you are working
on the form. The dashboard is for everything you do with all of them at once.
